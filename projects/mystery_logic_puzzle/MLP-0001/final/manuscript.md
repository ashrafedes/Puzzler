## C01 — The Gallery Theft
*Category: theft · Difficulty: easy*

Three pieces vanished from the Marlowe Collection overnight. Security saw each suspect in exactly one room.

Match each suspect to the item they took and the room where they were seen.

**Clues**

1. Ada IS matched with statue
2. Ada IS matched with gallery
3. Bram IS matched with painting
4. Bram is NOT matched with gallery
5. Cora IS matched with lobby

**Hints**
- Clue 1 pins Ada's item directly.
- With Ada in the gallery and Cora in the lobby, only the vault remains for Bram.

## C09 — The Estate Auction
*Category: theft · Difficulty: hard*

Four collectors bid on seized contraband at a closed estate auction. The clerk's notes are scrambled — reconstruct who won what, with which paddle, paid how.

Match each bidder to their lot, paddle, and payment method.

**Clues**

1. at least one of: (Kest ↔ check) , (Kest ↔ vase)
2. at least one of: (Kest ↔ 21) , (Jago ↔ 34)
3. at least one of: (Kest ↔ coin) , (Liv ↔ check)
4. at least one of: (Jago ↔ 21) , (Liv ↔ mask)
5. at least one of: (map ↔ wire) , (12 ↔ cash)
6. at least one of: (Liv ↔ mask) , (mask ↔ crypto)
7. exactly one of: (34 ↔ cash) , (map ↔ 21)
8. exactly one of: (mask ↔ 34) , (Iris ↔ 12)
9. exactly one of: (Iris ↔ vase) , (7 ↔ check)
10. exactly one of: (vase ↔ cash) , (Kest ↔ mask)

**Hints**
- Every alternative to Liv holding paddle 21 leads to a contradiction — trace each branch.
- Assume Kest holds the mask: the xor chains collapse. So the coin is his lot.

## C10 — The Harvest Ledger
*Category: smuggling · Difficulty: hard*

Four vintners moved contraband through a cooperative: each hid it in one varietal, aged it in one barrel, and shipped it in one season. The cellar ledger is encoded.

Match each vintner to their grape, barrel, and shipping season.

**Clues**

1. exactly one of: (clay ↔ spring) , (spring ↔ Yael)
2. at least one of: (spring ↔ Xiu) , (summer ↔ Vera)
3. exactly one of: (riesling ↔ oak) , (riesling ↔ clay)
4. at least one of: (steel ↔ Xiu) , (chestnut ↔ Yael)
5. at least one of: (muscat ↔ Wade) , (chestnut ↔ Wade)
6. at least one of: (steel ↔ autumn) , (steel ↔ spring)
7. at least one of: (muscat ↔ Xiu) , (summer ↔ Yael)
8. exactly one of: (gamay ↔ steel) , (clay ↔ Yael)

**Hints**
- Try placing spring with Yael — every alternative in the xor chain collapses.
- Branch on Vera's barrel: only steel survives the or/xor cascade.
