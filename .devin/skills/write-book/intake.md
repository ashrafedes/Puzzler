# Intake — One Consolidated Round, Focused Blocker Follow-up Only

First read existing answers and user-provided context. Present the form below once,
with the **recommended default** shown for each non-essential item so the user can
reply "defaults" for any line. Do not make the user repeat known information.
Record in `intake_answers.md` under separate headings: STATED (verbatim),
ACCEPTED_DEFAULT (user said to use the default), ASSUMPTIONS, OPEN_ISSUES.
Never label an inferred answer as a user statement. Unresolved essentials block
dependent work; optional items may be skipped.

## A. Essentials (no default; answer required)

1. Working title and optional subtitle.
2. Nonfiction or fiction; subject and reader promise, or genre and premise.
3. Language and regional variant.
4. Required final print page count (acceptance range is ±5%).
5. Binding (paperback or hardcover), trim size, paper (white/cream) and interior
   ink (black or color). Defaults if undecided: paperback, 6x9 in, cream, black.
6. Audience, their prior knowledge, and the practical outcome (or reading experience).
7. Author name as it should appear, and what the author can truthfully supply:
   real experiences, data, documents, past work (Q9, Q11).

## B. Sources, stories and permissions

8. Source files/URLs and author materials. The pipeline may add verified research
   but cannot manufacture personal experience.
9. Which specific personal accounts are supplied or confirmed. Default: none; the
   pipeline writes no first-person war stories and implies no witnessed events.
10. May clearly labeled illustrative scenarios fill teaching gaps? Default: yes,
    labeled where used, including their tables and charts.
11. Confidentiality, anonymization, quotation/image rights, and limits on sending
    manuscript text to external services. Default: nothing leaves this session.
12. Review path: (a) separate session you open, (b) authorized external model with
    budget/privacy limits, or (c) waive independent review (final status will say
    so; see `SKILL.md`). Default: (a). Also: paid services budget and subagent
    permission. Default: none.

## C. Design and deliverables

13. Voice/person (fiction: POV and tense). Default: second person, direct, plain.
14. Illustration tier: none, diagrams/tables, chapter headers, or fully illustrated;
    style, color limits, accessibility. Default: diagrams/tables only, code-rendered.
15. Callouts, worked examples, worksheets, downloads. Companion links/QRs need a
    real approved destination or are omitted. Default: worked examples plus
    reusable templates inside the book, no QR codes.
16. Imprint, copyright holder/year, dedication, author bio (verified text only),
    ISBN plan (free KDP ISBN or your own). Defaults: author as holder, current
    year, KDP-assigned ISBN, no dedication.
17. Deliverables: print interior PDF, cover wrap (art supplied or generated),
    editable DOCX, ebook, KDP metadata file. Default: interior PDF, cover wrap, metadata.
18. Price target, categories, keywords, series, territories. Optional. Note the
    pipeline will check KDP's current list-price limits and minimum price for the
    chosen trim and page count; a target outside them is recorded as an open issue.
19. Comparable books, content limits, scope exclusions. Optional.
20. Acknowledgement: you will answer KDP's AI-content disclosure questions truthfully
    when you publish (the pipeline records what was AI-generated, but you submit it).

## After intake

Record agreed facts and unresolved items. Check current KDP support for the selected
binding/trim/paper/ink/page combination (page limits, trim availability,
price limits); unsupported combinations need user resolution, not silent
substitution. Do not assume paperback formulas work for hardcover. Identify which
research can proceed without blocked inputs.

Continue without routine approval requests between passed stages. If a later
accuracy, rights, safety, author-source, billing or essential-scope issue emerges,
ask only the focused blocking question, record BLOCKED and pause affected work.
The consolidated-intake preference never authorizes invented answers, fabricated
provenance, concealed uncertainty or bypassing a required permission.
